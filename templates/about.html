How these numbers are made

This app uses two kinds of models. For the things a public survey dataset actually measured, we use a statistical model trained on that dataset. For the things the dataset does not contain, we built our own research-based model from published sun-exposure rules. We keep the two separate on purpose, and we show which is which.

It is an educational project. It is not a medical device, not a blood test and not a diagnosis.

What it does not know

Diet, genetics, season and weather, clothing in detail, how much shade you really stand in, and the real response of your own body. Sun time shown is a best case. The survey only tells us patterns across many people. Please check your real level with a blood test and follow your doctor's advice.
